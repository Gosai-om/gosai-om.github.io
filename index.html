Authentication & access-control hardening

Security
FastAPIRBACOTP step-upSecure cookiesRate limiting
Challenge
Permissions were checked inconsistently, sensitive actions needed stronger proof of identity, and new members received passwords by email.
What I built
Reusable roles with per-member scope enforced on the server, step-up OTP for sensitive actions, invite links instead of emailed passwords, plus rate limiting and stricter CORS and cookie settings.
Outcome
Server-enforced permissions on every protected endpoint, and onboarding that never sends a password.

FAQ

Common questions

Can you work on a codebase someone else wrote?

Yes, that's most of my work. I start by running the project locally and reading through the code, then fix the most urgent issue first. If the project has no documentation, I write a short setup and architecture note as I go.

Will you sign an NDA and work white-label?

Yes. Your client sees your agency, not me. I don't contact your clients and I don't show your project in my portfolio without written permission.

How do you charge?

A fixed price per task, an hourly rate for ongoing overflow work, or a monthly maintenance block. Send me the task and I'll reply with an estimate before any work starts.

What do you need from us to start?

Repository access (a limited-access account is fine), a way to run the project or a staging environment, and the ticket or a short description of the problem.

Which technologies do you not cover?

My core is Python (FastAPI) and Angular, with MySQL, PostgreSQL, Redis, Celery, Docker and AWS. I can handle smaller React Native tasks. For PHP, WordPress or .NET work I'll tell you honestly that someone else is a better fit.

How fast can you start?

Send the details and your timeline. I'll confirm availability and a start date before you commit to anything.
